Privacy Policy

Floory ("the App") values your privacy and complies with applicable data protection laws. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

To provide automatic parking location and underground floor detection features, the App collects and processes the following information:

  1. Location Data (GPS & Background Location)
    • Purpose: To detect when driving ends and record your parking location (latitude and longitude).
    • Processing: Processed and stored locally on your device (Local DB). Location data is not sold or shared with third parties.
  2. Sensor Data (Barometer, Accelerometer, Activity Recognition)
    • Purpose: To detect transitions from driving to walking and analyze barometric pressure changes across underground parking levels to determine the parked floor.
    • Processing: Analyzed in real time on your device and stored locally.
  3. Diagnostics & Performance Data (Firebase)
    • Items Collected: Device identifiers, crash logs, and service stability metrics.
    • Purpose: To fix bugs, improve floor detection accuracy, and monitor app stability via Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

2. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your parking history, saved places, and barometric sensor records are stored locally on your device.
  • If you delete records within the App or uninstall the App, all local data is immediately and permanently deleted.

3. Sharing of Information with Third Parties

  • The App does not sell or share your personal or location information with any third parties.
  • For crash reporting and service analytics, the App uses Google Firebase services (Google LLC), which process data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Your Rights and Permissions

You can revoke or modify location and physical activity permissions at any time in your device's Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Floory ➔ Permissions. (Note: Automatic floor detection may be limited if required permissions are denied.)
